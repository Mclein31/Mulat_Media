Core Values

What We Won't Compromise On

"Eight principles that guide every editorial decision we make — no matter the deadline, the story, or the pressure to publish first."

Everything MULAT MEDIA publishes passes through the same eight values. They aren't aspirational language for a mission statement — they're the standard our editors check every story against before it goes out, and the standard we hold ourselves to when we get something wrong.

Our Eight Core Values
01

Integrity

We uphold truth and accuracy through responsible fact-checking, ethical reporting, and avoiding sensationalism. We are committed to presenting facts responsibly and without bias.

02

Collaboration

We believe that teamwork and diverse perspectives create stronger and more meaningful journalism. We work together to inform and serve the student community and the public.

03

Innovation

We embrace digital media, emerging technologies, and creative storytelling formats to keep our audience informed, connected, and engaged.

04

Accountability

We take responsibility for the information we publish. We ensure that our reports are accurate, fair, and based on verified information.

05

Transparency

We value accurate and honest reporting. We make sure that the information we share is based on facts and responsible journalism.

06

Service

Mulat Media exists to serve its audience, not just to produce content. We want our work to help students understand what is happening around them and give them useful information they can actually apply to campus life and their communities.

07

Student Empowerment

Mulat Media exists to serve its audience, not just to produce content. We want our work to help students understand what is happening around them and give them useful information they can actually apply to campus life and their communities.

08

Community Awareness

Being mulat means understanding that campus life does not exist separately from the world outside school. Issues involving technology, education, misinformation, employment, local communities, and Filipino youth can influence students directly or indirectly. We want our stories to help students see those connections.

Values We Hold Each Other To

These eight values aren't just printed on a page — they're what every new contributor is onboarded on, and what every editor checks a story against before it's published. When we fall short, we say so.

They're also what makes MULAT MEDIA a publication our readers can trust issue after issue, regardless of who's on the byline.

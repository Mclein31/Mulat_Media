Our Beginning

A small idea that grew into a full newsroom.

MULAT MEDIA began as a shared interest among a handful of students.

"Mulat" is a Filipino word associated with being: Awake, Aware, Conscious of what is happening around us

While "Media" represents our different ways of sharing information: Broadcasting, Social media, Video, Photography, Digital stories

It's goal is to open people's eyes through responsible media, and to look beyond headlines, ask questions, and understand the story.

How the Newsroom Works
01

Report

Writers and photographers gather stories from across campus — verifying sources, checking facts, and following leads with care.

02

Edit

Every draft passes through our editorial team, where clarity, fairness, and accuracy are checked before a single word goes public.

03

Publish

Designers and editors bring each story to its final form, shaping it into an issue our readers can trust and return to.

Who Makes Mulat Media

A community built on communication and craft.

"Every byline here belongs to someone who cared enough to get the story right."

MULAT MEDIA brings together a community of individuals committed to communication, storytelling, creativity, and responsible media. Writers, photographers, designers, and editors each bring their own craft to the newsroom, working under one shared editorial standard.

We're a training ground as much as we are a publication — a place where students build real skills in journalism, design, and media literacy while producing work that matters to the community around them.

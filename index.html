About the Publication

A platform built around communication and craft.

"We believe good journalism starts with people who care enough to get it right."

MULAT MEDIA brings together a community of individuals committed to communication, storytelling, creativity, and responsible media. What began as a shared interest in student journalism has grown into a full editorial organization with dedicated writers, photographers, designers, and editors.

Every member contributes to a publication built on accuracy, curiosity, and craft — one story, one issue, one voice at a time.

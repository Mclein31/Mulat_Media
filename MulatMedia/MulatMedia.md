# 📰 MulatMedia

**MulatMedia** is the official digital publication platform and newsroom content management system (CMS) engineered for **Mulat Media**, an active student journalism publication. Built with semantic HTML5, modular CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, and powered by a cloud Supabase PostgreSQL database, it manages the full editorial lifecycle: student draft submissions, editorial peer review, Editor-in-Chief publishing authorizations, daily interactive Wordle games, and live reader metrics.

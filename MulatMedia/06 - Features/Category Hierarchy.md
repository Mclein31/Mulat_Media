# Category Hierarchy

Organized into 6 distinct sections:
- `news-current-affairs`: Breaking university news.
- `opinion`: Editorials and commentaries.
- `sports`: Athletic game coverage.
- `entertainment`: Pop culture and arts.
- `metrostyle`: Student lifestyle and fashion.
- `campus-press`: Student government and clubs.

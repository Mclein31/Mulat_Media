Mission

Our Purpose, In Plain Words

"Mulat Media's mission is to inform, empower, and connect the Student community through responsible student journalism and multimedia broadcasting."

To inform and engage students and communities through accurate, accessible, and entertaining digital news. We aim to amplify the voices of Filipino students, develop their skills in responsible journalism, and connect campus issues to important national conversations. Lastly, our main mission is to make our platform not exclusive to ourselves, but open to all aspiring and responsible citizens of the future, giving everyone an opportunity to learn, participate, and share their voices.

How We Carry It Out
01

Report Responsibly

We verify before we publish. Every story is checked for accuracy, context, and fairness before it reaches a reader.

02

Represent Fairly

We seek out perspectives beyond our own newsroom, so our coverage reflects the full range of the community we report on.

03

Engage Meaningfully

We publish to start conversations, not end them — inviting readers to respond, question, and get involved.

Journalism With a Purpose

Behind every issue is a team asking the same question: does this serve our readers? That question shapes what we cover, how we cover it, and why we keep showing up to newsroom meetings long after the deadline has passed.

Our mission isn't a statement we wrote once and forgot — it's the standard every contributor is trained on and held to, issue after issue.
